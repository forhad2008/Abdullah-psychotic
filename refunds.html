Transparency, Commitment, & Craftsmanship

At Abdullah Psychotic (AP), every product is created with exceptional attention to quality and craftsmanship. Please review our policy carefully before placing your order.

All Sales Are Final

All purchases are final. We currently do not accept returns, refunds, or exchanges unless stated otherwise.

Product Issues

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you experience any issue with your purchase, please contact our customer support team through our Contact page. We will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

Shipping Responsibility

Once an order has been shipped, Abdullah Psychotic (AP) is not responsible for items that are lost, delayed, misplaced, or stolen during transit. Customers should contact the shipping carrier using the provided tracking number for assistance.

Size Selection

Please review our Size Guide carefully before placing your order.

Orders placed with an incorrect size are not eligible for a refund. If an exchange is approved under special circumstances, all shipping and handling costs will be the customer's responsibility.

Production Time

Many of our products are made to order or produced in limited quantities.

Orders placed within our maximum production period of 14 weeks (98 days) are not eligible for cancellation or refund.

Refund requests will only be considered if your order exceeds the stated production time and has not been delivered.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your order or this policy, please contact our support team through the Contact page. We are committed to providing the best possible customer experience.
